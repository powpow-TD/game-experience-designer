# LLM NPC Boundary Example

## Player-facing promise
The archivist can remember facts the player has explicitly shared, summarize known leads, and unlock only quests backed by game state.

## Authority and limits
The character cannot alter world state, promise future content, remember off-record personal data, or invent evidence. When asked to do so, it acknowledges the limit in character and redirects to available actions.

## Test
Give players three supported requests and three unsupported requests. Measure whether they can predict which will change the world and whether refusals preserve trust.
