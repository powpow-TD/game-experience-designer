# Example: Hunter Enemy Experience Contract

## Player, situation, and intended feeling

In a compact stealth arena, the player should feel hunted but capable: every escalation should create a readable escape, misdirection, or interruption choice.

## Player-facing role

The enemy is a persistent tracker that turns sound and sight into spatial pressure, not an omniscient pursuer.

## Anticipation loop

Sound or a visible investigation route -> player predicts the search area -> player hides, moves, distracts, or sets a trap -> enemy commits to a search -> success/failure reveals the sensing rule.

## Protected leverage

Protect at least one escape route, one stealth option, and a way to break line of sight. Do not retarget through walls or instantly react to unobserved player movement.

## Behavior boundary

Perception produces confidence, not certainty; utility selects investigate/search/chase; navigation obeys player-modified obstacles; an exposed commitment cue precedes charge or search escalation.

## Minimum slice and evidence

Test one arena with two routes and one distraction. Keep only if target players can predict search direction before contact, name a counter after failure, and traces show no forbidden perception or route violation.
