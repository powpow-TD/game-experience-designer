# AI Companion Specification Example

## Player-facing role
The companion makes the player feel supported under pressure without taking tactical initiative away.

## Observable states and readable cues
- **Following**: stays behind the player; relaxed body language.
- **Claiming cover**: points or calls the destination before movement.
- **Yielding**: steps aside when the player approaches a reserved escape route.
- **Recovering**: announces loss of path or target and returns to a safe follow behavior.

## Test
In a two-cover greybox encounter, measure whether players can explain the companion's intent and retain an escape route after an enemy push.
