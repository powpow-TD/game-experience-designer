# Playtest Protocol Example

**Question**: Can players read an enemy's charge before the outcome matters?  
**Scene**: A thirty-second greybox arena with one charge enemy.  
**Task**: Survive two charges without tutorial text.  
**Observe**: First reaction, dodge timing, verbal explanation, and post-failure strategy.  
**Decision rule**: If fewer than 70% can explain the cue after a second attempt, revise the signal before increasing damage or speed.
