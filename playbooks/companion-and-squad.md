# Companion, Squad, and Bot AI

## Experience promise

Choose the role: reassuring partner, coordinated specialist, believable teammate, practice opponent, or social glue. Do not define the companion as "optimal." Its first responsibility is the player's initiative and readable cooperation.

## Priority order

1. Protect player survival, escape route, current interaction, camera space, and tactical authorship.
2. Make the companion's commitment visible before it consumes shared space, target, or resource.
3. Yield, acknowledge, and recover when a conflict occurs.
4. Optimize local tactical value only inside those constraints.

## Coordination contract

Define shared knowledge, callouts, reservation ownership, formation/spacing, target claims, friendly-fire boundaries, latency tolerance, and failure behavior. For multiplayer bots, also define team and match incentives; local bot competence must not damage competitive integrity.

## Trust tests

Track route conflicts, blocked interactions, accidental exposure, duplicated targets, unexplained departures, recovery success, and player attribution of failure. A good companion is one whose limitations are understandable and whose help does not feel like theft of play.
