# Adaptive Difficulty, Pacing, and AI Director

## Choose the adaptation target

Adapt a specific variable: encounter pressure, information, timing, support, pacing, content route, or practice opportunity. Do not define the goal as "keep the player engaged" without a protected competence model.

## Contract

| Field | Define |
|---|---|
| Signal | evidence of player state and its uncertainty |
| Knobs | variables the director may change |
| Protected outcomes | achievements, rankings, stakes, or learning that cannot be silently rewritten |
| Bounds | max magnitude, rate, and duration of adjustment |
| Disclosure | what the player should know or be able to control |
| Stop / rollback | conditions that disable or reverse adaptation |

## Pacing in systemic content

For procedural levels or open worlds, use event density, encounter spacing, spatial opportunity, and transition cues to shape anticipation. Preserve authored-feeling causal chains: a player should still be able to explain why pressure rose or relief arrived.

## Validate fairness

Compare informed and uninformed players, inspect replay traces, test edge cases, and check whether players attribute success and failure to their own decisions. If adaptation erases learning or agency, reduce its authority rather than hiding it better.
