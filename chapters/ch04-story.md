# Chapter 4: Story

## Core idea

Interactive story works when player action, character motivation, and world response agree.

## Practical method

Audit what the player is rewarded for against what the fiction claims to value.

## Game-AI application

NPC goals, memory, dialogue, and available actions must make the same promise.

## Review prompts

- What player expectation is this system creating?
- What evidence can a player perceive before the consequence matters?
- What smallest test could prove this reasoning wrong?
