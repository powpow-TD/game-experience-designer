# Chapter 15: Drive

## Core idea

Teams sustain quality through meaningful progress, real feedback, and manageable challenge.

## Practical method

Break work into playable capability slices and bring player evidence back to the makers.

## Game-AI application

A working AI behavior slice is more motivating than a large invisible backlog.

## Review prompts

- What player expectation is this system creating?
- What evidence can a player perceive before the consequence matters?
- What smallest test could prove this reasoning wrong?
