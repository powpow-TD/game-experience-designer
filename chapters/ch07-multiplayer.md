# Chapter 7: Multiplayer

## Core idea

Social play amplifies cooperation, competition, mind games, skill gaps, and harmful incentives.

## Practical method

Separate individual, team, and match-level incentives; look for destructive local optima.

## Game-AI application

Design bots and team AI to support shared goals without becoming invisible back-seat drivers.

## Review prompts

- What player expectation is this system creating?
- What evidence can a player perceive before the consequence matters?
- What smallest test could prove this reasoning wrong?
