# Chapter 1: AI Experience Intent

## Player-experience principle

Define the emotional promise of the AI from the player's point of view, not its algorithmic capability.

## Design method

Specify player situation, intended feeling, AI role, visible evidence, and failure boundary.

## Questions for an AI technical designer

- What will the player infer about this AI before the outcome occurs?
- What authority, limitation, and fallback should be visible?
- Which evidence would make us revise this behavior?
