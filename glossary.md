# AI Game Experience Glossary

- **Agency budget**: The amount of player choice an AI intervention is allowed to automate or constrain.
- **Failure contract**: The visible rules for an AI feature's failure, fallback, and player recovery.
- **Intent surface**: Player-perceivable evidence of an AI actor's state, goal, and commitment.
- **LLM authority boundary**: The executable limits on a language-driven character's memory, promises, and world actions.
- **Player-facing role**: The experiential job an AI actor performs for the player.
- **Protected achievement**: A result that adaptive systems must not secretly invalidate.
