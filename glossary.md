# AI Game Experience Glossary

- **Agency budget**: the player choice an AI intervention is allowed to automate or constrain.
- **Anticipation loop**: cue, player forecast, response, resolution, and learned rule around an AI moment.
- **Authority boundary**: executable limits on an AI/LLM character's knowledge, memory, claims, tools, and world actions.
- **Belief/state**: what an agent currently treats as true, distinct from the full world state.
- **Commitment window**: the readable period after intent is set and before a consequential action resolves.
- **Experience contract**: a testable statement of player role, leverage, cues, limits, recovery, and evidence for an AI feature.
- **Failure contract**: detectable failure, fallback, player recovery, and explanation for an AI feature.
- **Fidelity envelope**: the level of simulation detail permitted while preserving player-visible causal rules.
- **Legibility**: a player's ability to form a useful explanation of AI behavior.
- **Leverage**: meaningful player prediction, influence, counterplay, recovery, or authorship around an AI action.
- **Player-facing role**: the experiential job an AI performs, such as pressure, support, discovery, or world credibility.
- **Protected outcome**: an achievement, stake, or learning result adaptation must not silently invalidate.
- **Reservation**: an explicit claim on a spatial, temporal, or interaction resource used to avoid AI/player contention.
