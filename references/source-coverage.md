## How coverage is used

The Game AI Pro material supplies the implementation and production vocabulary; the experience-design material supplies the evaluation lens. The skill promotes only cross-cutting principles that can be tested in a modern project, routes specific work to playbooks, and marks historical or unsafe patterns in `scope-and-modernization.md`.
