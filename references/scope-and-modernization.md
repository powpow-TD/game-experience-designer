# Scope and Modernization Policy

## Retain

Retain the durable reasoning in classic game-AI material: decision ownership, behavior selection, perception uncertainty, path and space reasoning, tactical queries, coordination, simulation fidelity, testing, debugging, and player-facing fairness. Re-express every item as a current product constraint and validate it in the target engine.

## Treat as historical or conditional

- Exact CPU, memory, frame-time, and scalability claims from older hardware.
- Engine, compiler, STL, scripting, or rendering integration advice tied to a specific production stack.
- Early neural-network and evolutionary examples used as universal answers rather than scoped tools.
- Hidden rubber-banding or opaque difficulty adjustment that protects a metric at the expense of earned outcomes.
- Algorithm-first recommendations that omit player readability, accessibility, online safety, privacy, or operational ownership.

## Exclude from this public skill

- Copyrighted prose, scans, OCR, figures, tables, code, and large excerpts from source books or talks.
- Undated benchmark numbers and vendor-specific implementation recipes that cannot be verified in the target project.
- Design patterns that rely on deception, coercive retention, or unbounded character/LLM promises.

## Current requirements added to classic material

Add authority boundaries for generative AI, privacy and consent for player data, safety escalation, accessibility, model/tool observability, rollback, and human ownership. A system is not production-ready merely because its behavior looks intelligent.
