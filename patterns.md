# AI Experience Patterns

## Intent Surface
Expose an AI actor's consequential goal through movement, animation, space, sound, and restrained UI.

## Player-Priority Reservation
Reserve critical player cover, escape routes, interaction targets, and decision moments before optimizing companion behavior.

## Graceful Boundary
When an NPC or LLM cannot execute a request, acknowledge the limit, preserve character voice, and route the player to real actions.

## Honest Adaptation
Adapt pressure or support without secretly rewriting an outcome the player believes they earned.

## Failure Contract
Specify detectable failure, fallback behavior, recovery path, and post-failure explanation for every consequential AI feature.
