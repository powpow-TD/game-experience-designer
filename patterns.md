# Patterns

## Intent Surface
Make an AI goal observable through movement, animation, space, sound, and restrained UI.

## Player-Priority Reservation
Reserve critical escape routes, cover, and interaction space for the player before optimizing companion behavior.

## Hypothesis Loop
Turn an experience claim into one minimum scene, measurable signals, and a keep/change/kill decision.

## Evidence Ledger
Separate observations, telemetry, interviews, inferences, and assumptions in every design decision.
